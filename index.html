About Me

I am a business process and analytics professional with experience spanning financial services, technology risk, internal controls, and business strategy. My work focuses on improving how organizations understand performance, manage risk, and make informed decisions.

My professional background includes process ownership and protective security at USAA, technology risk consulting at Ernst & Young, and financial audit and internal control work supporting the Defense Health Agency. These experiences have shaped my interest in practical analytics: not simply building models, but making sure the results are reliable, explainable, and useful to decision makers.

I hold a master's degree in International Relations with a concentration in Security Policy and a bachelor's degree in History with a minor in Business. I am pursuing a Master of Science in Data Science at Bellevue University. My portfolio brings together applied machine learning, statistical analysis, data visualization, and real business questions.
